WHO WE ARE

About AEEE

AEEE is the student association of the Electrical and Electronics Engineering department at Government Engineering College Wayanad. We bring students together through technical workshops, events, projects and collaborative experiences that go beyond the classroom. Our goal is to learn, build and lead together.

Learn

Hands-on workshops, expert talks and peer-led sessions.

Build

Student projects, competitions and technical showcases.

Lead

Execom roles that develop teamwork and organising skills.
